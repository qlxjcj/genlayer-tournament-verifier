Prize Escrow (GEN)

1 · Create Tournament

Prize pool is escrowed in the contract. Each participant needs a real payout address (0x + 40 hex).

2 · Submit Match Result

Two distinct source URLs are required. Sources are cross-validated; empty, malformed or error responses fail closed.

3 · Settle / Refund

Settlement consumes a verified result and pays the winner's registered address. Cancel refunds the organizer.

4 · Inspect on-chain record

Records are bound to their own IDs — never to a shared "latest" pointer.
